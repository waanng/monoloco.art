ABOUT MONOLOCO

Three people,
a shared way of seeing。

Founded in 2017, monoloco is a three-person art collective. We began with community projects and now work across exhibitions and object design. We’re also developing a series of hands-on workshops.

Film, sketches and handmade notebooks help us pay attention to the places we live and visit. We’re interested in what happens next: how a photograph becomes a conversation, a drawing becomes an object, or an idea becomes something we make together.

Film, travel and things that grow

LZ

LZ comes from a media background and loves exploring a place through its everyday life—especially its markets. Film photography is a way to remember those encounters. Closer to home, gardening, composting and permaculture feed a growing interest in the land and how we care for it.

A camera, a bicycle, an eye for the city

KASA

KASA is a photographer and keen cyclist, attentive to the frame and to the city beyond it. The work follows changing urban spaces and scenes along the road, with an eye for the humour in everyday life.

Walking, looking, drawing

waanng

A self-taught urban sketcher, waanng draws whatever catches the eye along the way. Sketches, photographs and handmade notebooks become a record of everyday life and family travels. Some drawings go on to become objects people can use.

OUR PRACTICE

Rooted in the neighbourhood.
Open to what comes next.

We’ve made gardens in Beijing’s hutong neighbourhoods, put on exhibitions, sketched our walks and run a pop-up café. Each was a way to bring people together. Those experiences still shape what we make today.

  • Baitasi neighbourhood · a garden made with reused objects and the “Pharmacy” project
  • Hutong Art Museum · illustration exhibition
  • A Cup on the Move · CITYWALK travelling sketch exhibition
  • Xuanwumen hutong · monoloco pop-up café
