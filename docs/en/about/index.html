ABOUT MONOLOCO

Three people,
a shared way of seeing。

Founded in 2017, monoloco began with community projects and grew into curating, object design and an ongoing art practice.

We record cities through film, sketches and handmade notebooks, and bring creativity into everyday life by making things together. A shared question connects these explorations: how can an image keep living beyond the moment it was made?

Travel, film and growing things

LZ

With a background in media, LZ enjoys the local cultures encountered while travelling, especially markets. Film photography records those meetings, while gardening, composting and permaculture offer ways to explore a closer relationship with everyday life and the land.

OUR PRACTICE

Starting in our neighbourhood,
keeping creativity in motion.

We have made gardens in hutongs, organised exhibitions, recorded walks and opened a pop-up café as a place to meet. Threads from those experiences continue through today’s projects, workshops and objects.

  • Baitasi neighbourhood · a garden made with reused objects and the “Pharmacy” project
  • Hutong Art Museum · illustration exhibition
  • A Cup on the Move · CITYWALK travelling sketch exhibition
  • Xuanwumen hutong · monoloco pop-up café
