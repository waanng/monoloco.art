DESIGNED OBJECT

UCAS Campus Cup。

Hold a memory of campus in your hands.

A hand-drawn campus scroll becomes an everyday object. Scenes from the Yuquanlu, Zhongguancun and Yanqihu campuses of the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS) find a new setting on a cup and its packaging.

Cup graphics & packaging designDESIGN STUDY
01 / BEGINNINGS

A return to campus

Years after graduating, waanng returned to three UCAS campuses and recorded their buildings and scenery in a long painted scroll. Familiar architecture and paths became the starting point for this design.

02 / TRANSLATION

Bringing buildings out of the scroll

Distinctive buildings are selected and redrawn, translating a continuous campus landscape into graphics that can be read around a cup.

03 / CUP

An everyday object, carrying a memory

Architectural images and text meet on the cup. The material direction uses coffee grounds, connecting campus memories with a theme of reuse.

04 / PACKAGING

Three campuses, one continuous landscape

Campus buildings become a continuous sequence of geometric forms, extending the way a scroll is viewed into the packaging. This image shows a detail of the packaging artwork.
