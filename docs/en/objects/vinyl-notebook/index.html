DESIGNED OBJECT

Vinyl Travel Notebook。

Follow the record. Go somewhere new.

Carry the lines of a city and the memory of a piece of music in a notebook. Starting with the form of a record player, this design brings travel, music and personal notes together on paper.

Notebook & paper goods designDESIGN STUDY
01 / COVER

From record player to notebook

Simple shapes reinterpret the turntable, tonearm and controls. In the deep-coffee colour study, BEYOND THE NOTES plays on both musical notes and the notes a person writes.

02 / OPENING PAGE

Leave a place for the person using it

The centre label of a record becomes a space for a name, series number and dates of use. It opens the notebook while leaving its story for the owner to complete.

03 / CITIES & NOTES

Seven cities. Seven musical memories.

City sketches fit inside the circle of a record, creating inserts that connect place and music. Turn the image over and a blank page awaits a new record.

04 / DETAILS

From the cover to the paper band.

Travel routes, words and visual cues from records come together in a notebook made to accompany a journey.

Theme
Music, city journeys and personal notes
Elements
Cover, opening page, seven city inserts and a wraparound band
Shown here
A deep-coffee colour design study

A notebook to hold one journey, with room for the next.
