OBJECT DESIGN

Vinyl Travel Notebook。

A travel notebook inspired by vinyl records

A record player becomes the starting point for a travel notebook. Turntable graphics, city sketches and space for personal notes bring music and travel together on the page.

Notebook designDESIGN STUDY
01 / COVER

A record player, reimagined

The turntable, tonearm and controls become a simple graphic composition. BEYOND THE NOTES connects the notes we hear with the notes we write. Shown here in a dark coffee-brown colourway.

02 / INSIDE COVER

This one belongs to…

A record’s centre label becomes a place for the owner’s name, notebook number and dates. The design sets the scene; the person using it writes the rest.

03 / CITIES & NOTES

Seven cities, with music along the way.

Each circular insert pairs a city sketch with the shape of a vinyl record. On the reverse, there’s room for your own notes.

04 / DETAILS

Wrapped for the road.

The paper wrap brings the travel routes and record-inspired graphics together, ready to take on the road.

Inspired by
Music, travel and city sketches
Design includes
Cover, inside cover, seven city inserts and a paper wrap

For the journey you’re on, and the one still to come.
