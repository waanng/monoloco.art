HANDS-ON WORKSHOP

Film Developing Workshop。

From the shutter to the negative.

What happens between taking a photograph and seeing it? This workshop will introduce black-and-white film development, following the steps that turn an exposed roll into negatives.

Coming soonBlack-and-white film / Darkroom processes
TAKE PART

Look closely.
Then try it yourself.

Learn what happens to a roll of film after it leaves the camera. Hands-on activities and take-home materials will be confirmed for each session.

We’re still planning this workshop. We’ll announce who it’s for, what to bring and where it will take place when the details are confirmed.

01

Get to know film

Explore how a camera records an image on film and what happens after the shutter closes.

02

Inside the developing process

Learn about the equipment and main stages of black-and-white development. We’ll explain the hands-on activities when each session is announced.

03

Read the negative

Look at the developed film and consider how it becomes a photograph. Scanning or printing may be included, depending on the session.
