HANDS-ON WORKSHOP

Film Developing Workshop。

Watch a photographed moment slowly appear.

Between pressing the shutter and seeing a photograph, there is a process worth experiencing by hand. We plan to begin with black-and-white film and explore how a latent image becomes visible.

In preparationBlack-and-white film / Hands-on practice / Time
TAKE PART

Look closely.
Then try it yourself.

An understanding of the journey from exposure to a developed black-and-white negative. Hands-on activities and take-home results will be specified for each session.

This workshop is in preparation. Equipment, film requirements, intended participants and venue details will be announced with each session.

01

Begin with film

Get to know film and cameras, and explore how a photograph is recorded.

02

Approach the developing process

Learn about the main stages, equipment and working methods of black-and-white film development. The scope of hands-on participation will be specified for each session.

03

See the photograph again

Consider the relationship between photographs, negatives and memory. Whether scanning or enlarging is included will depend on the session.
