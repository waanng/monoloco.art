HANDS-ON WORKSHOP

Cyanotype Workshop。

Let light leave a shape.

Bring light, objects and paper together. Cyanotype is a photographic printing process that produces blue images. Use silhouettes, shadows and your own arrangement to make a print.

In preparationSunlight / Plants / Image-making
TAKE PART

Look closely.
Then try it yourself.

An encounter with light and shape, and a cyanotype print of your own.

Surfaces, materials and working arrangements depend on the session. Activities take place with on-site guidance.

01

Meet the cyanotype

Begin with the blue image and explore how light and a light-sensitive surface work together.

02

Arrange and wait

Choose objects, experiment with shadows and open space, and observe how the image changes in the light.

03

Watch the print appear

See the image emerge through washing and turn an experiment into a print of your own.

THE MAKING PROCESS

Light slowly leaves its trace.

These original hand-drawn diagrams introduce the cyanotype process. Materials and activities will follow each session’s plan and on-site guidance. The three stages below are preparing and coating, drying and arranging, and exposing and washing.
