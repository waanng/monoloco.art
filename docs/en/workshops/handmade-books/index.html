HANDS-ON WORKSHOP

Bookbinding Workshop。

Make a small notebook. Give everyday life a place.

Start with used wrapping paper and materials close at hand. Stitch or bind loose pages into a light, portable notebook, then fill it with photographs, sketches and words.

In preparationReused paper / Binding / Everyday records
TAKE PART

Look closely.
Then try it yourself.

A notebook made by your own hands, and a way to keep noticing and recording everyday life.

You can start collecting paper, packaging and photographs you like. Tools and materials will be specified for each session.

01

Begin with used paper

Find wrapping paper and cover materials worth using again. Prepare them for a small notebook you can carry with you.

02

Make a notebook of your own

Explore stitched, ring-bound and accordion structures. The binding method for each session will be described when it is announced.

03

How will you fill it?

Begin with a photograph, a route or a sentence. You do not have to wait until you can draw well: everyday life is already worth recording.

MAKE A BOOK

First, make a book.

Discover different ways to join loose pages. Each session will specify the book structure and materials used. The original diagrams show stitching, ring binding and accordion folding.

FILL IT WITH LIFE

What will you fill it with?

Start with wrapping paper saved by a friend. Cut it down and use materials close at hand for a cover. Small and light, the notebook goes wherever you go. Fill it without pressure: roads, views, shops and people slowly become a memory book of your own.
