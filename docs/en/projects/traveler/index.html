ART PROJECT / Travel photography

The Traveler。

Fragments of memory, held in photographs

Snapshots from a journey become fragments of memory. A street corner, a station, a cat or ripples on water may not identify a landmark, yet each can bring back the feeling of having been there.

LZSelect an image to view larger ↗

A photograph keeps the feeling of “I was there.”

Memory does not always arrange itself by place. It might return as someone outside a shop, a cat in an alley or a pause beside the water.

These images do not hurry to tell the whole journey. Unrelated scenes become neighbours, leaving room for their chance encounters to continue.

Adapted from issue one of our magazine Hudu (忽独), with credit to the original photographers and writers. Maps, handwriting and annotations in the artworks retain their original language.
