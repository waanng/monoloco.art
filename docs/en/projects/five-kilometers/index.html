ART PROJECT / Urban photography

Five Kilometers。

Beginning close to home

They say that what happens more than five kilometres from home no longer concerns you. This series starts nearby, looking at the connections between a city’s ring roads, buildings, interiors and people.

KASASelect an image to view larger ↗

Look again at the places you think you know.

As a city grows, the radius of everyday life changes with it. Familiar street life sits alongside new spaces. Buildings, roads and people make up a landscape that is always shifting.

Pause at a window, a reading room or a street corner. The photographs hold the distances, light and quiet that are easily overlooked in familiar places.

Adapted from issue one of our magazine Hudu (忽独), with credit to the original photographers and writers. Maps, handwriting and annotations in the artworks retain their original language.
