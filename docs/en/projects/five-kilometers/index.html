ART PROJECT / Urban photography

Five Kilometres。

A closer look at familiar places

How much do we notice within five kilometres of home? Starting with familiar surroundings, this series looks at the roads, buildings, rooms and people that shape daily life in the city.

KASAOpen an image for a closer look ↗

Look again at the places you know.

As the city grows, our daily routes change. Familiar streets give way to new spaces, and the places we thought we knew begin to look different.

A window, a reading room, a doorway: these photographs pause over the light and quiet of places we might otherwise pass without a second glance.
