ART PROJECT / Photography / Everyday observations

A Gardener’s Notes。

Finding our way back to the soil

A packet of seeds becomes a small experiment in everyday life. Soil, insects, compost and the patient wait for something to grow all leave their traces in these photographs.

LZSelect an image to view larger ↗

Coffee grounds and eggshells become something more.

It began with trying out different seeds. The harvest was not always what I imagined, but insects and plant diseases brought new questions: what do worms like to eat, and how can soil become healthier?

These notes follow small, recurring observations. Kitchen scraps become compost and seeds become plants, making our relationship with the ground tangible again.

Adapted from issue one of our magazine Hudu (忽独), with credit to the original photographers and writers. Maps, handwriting and annotations in the artworks retain their original language.
