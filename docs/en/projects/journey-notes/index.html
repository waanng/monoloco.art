ART PROJECT / Photography / Travel maps

The Three of Us。

Our family, on the road

The Three of Us is a family album made along the way: a walk through a new city, a shared meal, a place to sit, a handful of balloons. Photographs remember our time together; hand-drawn maps trace the journeys.

waanngOpen an image for a closer look ↗

A long road ahead. Each other to lean on.

We keep going. Sometimes we run ahead; sometimes we stop to rest. We look at each other and smile. There is a long road ahead, and we have each other to lean on.

Coasts and campuses, parks and backstreets, bookshops and galleries: the setting changes, but we are still us. Two people in front of the camera, one behind it. The three of us.

The maps show where we went. The photographs remember what it was like to be there together. This is less a list of destinations than an album of the moments in between.
