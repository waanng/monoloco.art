Notes from the Road。

Family journeys, drawn into memory

Travelling with family brings new scenery at every turn. Drawing the route, photographing encounters and filling a notebook keep those shared moments close.

waanngSelect an image to view larger ↗

The roads we take slowly become a map of our own.

Our first long family road trip took us to Key West. There was a church passed at midnight, a new year entered on the road and a map filled with names along the way.

Campuses in Texas, the coast along Highway 1, universities and bookshops encountered in different cities all appear beside the family in these photographs. A record of a journey holds not only where we went, but how we kept each other company.

Adapted from issue one of our magazine Hudu (忽独), with credit to the original photographers and writers. Maps, handwriting and annotations in the artworks retain their original language.
