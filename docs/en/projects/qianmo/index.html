ART PROJECT / On-site drawing / Maps / Stamps & postmarks

Qianmo。

Where Time and Place Meet

I stop to draw wherever I travel, placing the scene and a hand-drawn map on the same page. A postage stamp and a local postmark from that day give the moment its place and date. One sheet holds both a view and a record of being there.

waanngSelect an image to view larger ↗

Paths cross. Time and place meet on the page.

The drawing carries a feeling; the map locates it. Stamps and postmarks anchor that pause to a particular place and day. Held up against the scene itself, the sketchbook records both an encounter and the person who stood there.

Qianmo refers to intersecting paths through fields. Here, it also evokes the crossing of time and space, feeling and reason: colour and the trace of a hand meet maps, locations and dates. Small pauses along a journey become an archive that can be held and revisited.
