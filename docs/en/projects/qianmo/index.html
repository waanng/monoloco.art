ART PROJECT / Travel sketches / Maps / Postmarks

Qianmo。

Drawing a place, marking a moment

Wherever I travel, I stop to draw. A sketch and a map share the page; a stamp, postmarked locally that day, fixes it to a time and place. Each page becomes a record of where I was and what caught my eye.

waanngOpen an image for a closer look ↗

A place on the map. A moment on the page.

The sketch captures an impression; the map gives it a location. A stamp and a dated postmark add another kind of record. Photographed against the scene, the open sketchbook brings the drawing back into the world that prompted it.

Qianmo is a Chinese word for the paths that crisscross fields. For me, those crossings suggest other meeting points: time and place, intuition and reason. A drawing made in the moment sits beside the precision of a map and a date. Together, they offer different ways of remembering the same journey.
